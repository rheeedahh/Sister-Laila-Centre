What We Do

We build whatever truly helps.

SLC isn't limited to one type of programme. We create and run initiatives that respond to real needs, always keeping girls at the centre.

Our Approach

A broad range, driven by real need.

As needs arise in the community, SLC designs programmes across any of the following areas. This list will grow as we do.

✓

Education Support

Helping girls start, stay in and succeed through school.

✓

Digital & Practical Skills

Hands-on training that opens real, usable opportunities.

✓

Leadership & Mentorship

Building confidence and voice, girl to girl and mentor to mentee.

✓

Economic Empowerment

Skills and support that build independence over time.

✓

Health & Wellness

Support for wellbeing alongside learning and growth.

✓

Protection

Keeping girls safe as they learn, grow and take on new opportunities.

✓

Family & Community Engagement

Working with parents, boys and neighbours so change lasts.

✓

Advocacy

Speaking up for girls' education and opportunity beyond our own programmes.

+

And Beyond

New areas, as real community needs arise.
