About Us

Built on faith, vision, and community purpose.

Sister Laila Centre exists to remove the barriers that prevent girls in Boubai from completing their education and reaching their full potential.

In Loving Memory

The Legacy of Sister Laila

Sister Laila Centre was established to honor the life, vision, and selfless devotion of Late Sister Laila. Her lifelong conviction that educating a girl transforms an entire community serves as our guiding beacon.

"[Add a memorable quote or motto from Late Sister Laila here]"

Her spirit lives on through every student who steps into our classrooms, acquires digital skills, and steps forward with self-assurance.

Our Mission

Empowering every girl to succeed

We design and deliver grassroots programs that equip young women in Boubai with practical IT capabilities, personal leadership tools, and continuous mentorship. We actively work alongside boys, parents, and community leaders to foster a culture where girls' aspirations are nurtured and defended.

Our Vision

A community where no girl is left behind

We envision a Boubai where every young woman, regardless of financial standing, possesses the confidence, practical training, and community backing needed to pursue higher education, secure dignified employment, and lead with integrity.
